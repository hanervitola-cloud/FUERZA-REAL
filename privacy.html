Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you visit Earned Strength, how it's used, and what options you have.

Information I don't collect directly

Earned Strength doesn't ask you to register, has no user accounts, and doesn't directly collect your name, email, or personal data to browse the site.

The Earned Strength app

The Earned Strength app (on this site at /app and in app stores) has no ads. Everything you enter (your profile, workouts, records and chats) is stored on your own device. You can delete it at any time from Profile → Delete all my data.

Optional online account. If you turn on the online account (for rankings, live rooms, the AI coach and backup), the app's server (run on Cloudflare) stores: the name you choose, a friend code, your friends list, your best camera-challenge scores and, if you back up, a copy of your app data. There is no email and no password: your phone keeps a secret code (shown as your recovery code) and the server only stores a scrambled version of it. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google checks who you are and tells the server only the fixed user number of your Google account; the server keeps a scrambled version of that number so the same account opens on any phone. Your email, photo and other Google details are not stored. Your name and best scores appear in the rankings to other app users. You can delete the account and everything linked to it from Profile → Delete my online account.

AI coach. When you are online and have an account, your questions to the coach, the last few messages of the chat and a short profile (name, age, weight, goal, level and training days) are sent to an AI model run by Cloudflare Workers AI to write the answer. They are not stored by the app's server. Questions about pain, dizziness or feeling unwell are always answered by the app itself.

Camera. The camera is used only while you do a camera challenge. The images are processed on your device to find your body position and count your reps; they are never stored on a server or sent to anyone. The first time, the app downloads the pose-detection model from a public content network.

Videos. If you choose to record a video of a challenge, it stays on your device. You decide whether to save it or share it; the app never uploads it.

Live rooms. Live rooms run on the app's server: it passes each person's name and count to the others in the room while the room is open, and stores nothing after it closes (rooms close by themselves after 3 hours). If both people in a two-person room allow it, the camera picture goes directly between the two phones and does not pass through the server.

Challenge codes. A challenge code or link contains your name, the challenge and your score, and is only created when you choose to share it.

Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

Google's use of the advertising cookie enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you'll see a consent message provided by Google the first time you visit. There you can accept, refuse, or choose which kinds of advertising cookies you allow, and you can change that choice at any time from the same message.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's ad settings:

www.google.com/settings/ads

You can also review Google's privacy policy directly at:

policies.google.com/privacy

Browsing and analytics data

Like most websites, this site may automatically collect non-personal information such as browser type, operating system, referring page, and pages visited within the site, in order to understand how the content is used and improve it.

Third-party links

This site may also contain links to other external sites for educational and illustrative purposes. I'm not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites, so I recommend reviewing their privacy policies separately.

Minors

This site is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.
