Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you visit Earned Strength, how it's used, and what options you have.

Information we don't collect directly

Earned Strength doesn't ask you to register, has no user accounts, and doesn't directly collect your name, email, or personal data to browse the site.

Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display ads through Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of the advertising cookie enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you'll see a consent message provided by Google the first time you visit. There you can accept, refuse, or choose which kinds of advertising cookies you allow, and you can change that choice at any time from the same message.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's ad settings:

www.google.com/settings/ads

You can also review Google's privacy policy directly at:

policies.google.com/privacy

Browsing and analytics data

Like most websites, this site may automatically collect non-personal information such as browser type, operating system, referring page, and pages visited within the site, in order to understand how the content is used and improve it.

Third-party links

Some articles include embedded videos from the Earned Strength YouTube channel. They use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, so YouTube doesn't store cookies about your visit unless you play the video. Once you play it, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

This site may also contain links to other external sites for educational and illustrative purposes. We're not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites — we recommend reviewing their privacy policies separately.

Minors

This site is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this same page with the corresponding update date.

Contact
