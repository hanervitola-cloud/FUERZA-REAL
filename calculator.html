How it's calculated

•

Protein

We use 1.8 g per kg of body weight — a midpoint within the 1.6-2.2 g/kg range recommended for strength trainees. More detail in the complete protein guide.

•

Maintenance calories

Estimated by multiplying your weight by a factor based on your activity level (between 24 and 33 kcal/kg). For bulking, 15% is added; for cutting, 20% is subtracted. More context in bulking vs cutting.

This calculator provides a general estimate and does not replace the advice of a nutritionist, especially if you have any medical condition.
