How it is calculated

Protein

1.8 g per kg of bodyweight: the middle of the 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg range that research recommends for people who train for strength.

Calories

Your weight times a factor for how much you train (24 to 33 kcal per kg). To build muscle 15% is added; to lose fat 20% is taken off.

A general estimate, not a medical calculation. Adjust it after 2 or 3 weeks depending on how your body responds. If you have a medical condition, ask a nutritionist.
